#### The past

My primary background is in embedded systems. Writing C and HDL, designing PCBs, and verifying that the mess of wires and silicon on my desk produces a pretty signal on my CRT oscilloscope. It all started at age 8 with a knockoff Arduino Uno from Micro Center, and an array of LEDs I'd animate using `digitalWrite()`. The obsession continued to middle school when I developed a relay-driven smart home appliance centered around an ESP8266 which exposed an API to enable control from a mobile app. In high school and as an undergrad, I began to document more about the things I built. Check them out in [projects]({{< relref "projects" >}})! Once I became laser-focused on academics at Purdue though, notable works came from extensions on coursework artifacts. One of my favorites was the [Superhet FM digitally-tuned radio]({{< relref "projects/fm.md" >}}) I built in RF Communications in my sophomore year.
